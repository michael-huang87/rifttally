# Support

Found a bug, have a question, or want a feature? Email <a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps.

## Common questions

**A price looks wrong.** Prices come from public market data and update about once a day. A card with few sales can show an old or odd price. Email us the card name and set code if it looks badly off.

**A card won't scan.** Try more light, less glare, and holding the card flat and filling most of the frame. You can always add a card by searching for it.

**How do I set up deck chat?** Add a Google Gemini or Anthropic API key in the app's Profile tab. The key is stored on your device and only sent to that provider. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for what chat sends.

**How do I delete my data?** Delete a profile in the app to remove its collection, or delete the app to remove everything. We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

**Is this an official Riot Games app?** No. {{ site.app_name }} is an unofficial fan project. Riot Games does not endorse or sponsor it.
