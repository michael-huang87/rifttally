What it does

Scan with your camera

Hold up a card and {{ site.app_name }} names it, picks the right printing, and counts each copy as you scan. Recognition runs on your phone.

Live market prices

See what your collection is worth today, follow its value on a chart over time, and spot your biggest movers.

Sealed product

Track boxes and packs next to your cards, with what you paid, what they are worth now, and an expected value based on pull rates.

Log your pack openings

Opened real packs? Log them, scan the cards you pulled, and see whether the opening beat the sealed price. {{ site.app_name }} has no virtual pack opening.

Events

Keep upcoming and past events in one place, with your rounds, record, entry fees and prize packs.

PSA graded cards

Add your graded copies with their grade and cert number, and look up PSA population.

There are no accounts, ads, or analytics. Your collection stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
