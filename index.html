What it does

  • Scan cards with your camera to add them to your collection. Card recognition runs on your phone.
  • See what your collection is worth, with daily market prices and a value chart over time.
  • Search every card and sealed product, with prices for each printing and finish.
  • Build decks, check them against what you own, and log your matches.
  • Optionally look up graded PSA cards, or talk a deck through with an AI model, using your own API keys.

There are no accounts, ads, or analytics. Your collection stays on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
